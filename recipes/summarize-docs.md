---
name: summarize-docs
summary: summarize the documents in a directory
persona: technical-writer
args: dir
---
Summarize the documents in `{{dir}}`. List the files, read them, then write:

- a three-sentence overview of what the collection covers;
- one line per document: its name and its point;
- anything that contradicts across documents, is clearly out of date, or is missing.

Do not edit any file.
