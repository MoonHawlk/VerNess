name: plan
summary: turn any goal into a short, ordered plan
persona: project-manager
args: goal, deadline=none
---
Make a plan for: {{goal}}

Deadline: {{deadline}}.

Give: the outcome in one sentence; the steps in order, each with a rough effort and what it depends on; the two biggest risks with a mitigation each; and the first action to take today. Ask at most three questions, only if the answers would change the plan. Keep it to one screen.
